# Create: Air Logistics

**Create: Air Logistics** is a Create addon that lets you build hot-air airships capable of automatically transporting items and fluids between stations.

## Content
![Create: Air Logistics Items](docs/items.png)

- **Sky Dock:** The station where airships dock and transfer cargo, while also supplying the required air pressure.
- **Ship Port:** Placed on airships to connect and dock with a Sky Dock.
- **Pathfinder Propeller:** Steers the airship along the route plotted on a Flight Chart.
- **Boost Propeller:** Keeps heavier airships from slowing down.
- **Pressure Engine:** Connects to propellers and is the only block capable of powering them.
- **Pressure Tank:** Allows Pressure Engines to keep running over longer distances.
- **Airship Cargo Hold:** The storage block on airships used to hold items for transfer.
- **Airship Super Heater:** Heats Canvas Balloons much more effectively than campfires.
- **Flight Chart:** Used to plot 3D flight routes and inserted into a Pathfinder Propeller.
- **Wooden Propeller:** Crafting component required for Boost Propellers.
- **Mechanical Pathfinder:** Crafting component required for Pathfinder Propellers.
- **Canvas Balloon:** Essential for airship flight; provides lift when heated.

## Requirements
- **Minecraft:** `1.21.1`
- **NeoForge:** `21.1.209+`
- **Create:** `6.0.8+` (tested with `6.0.10`)
